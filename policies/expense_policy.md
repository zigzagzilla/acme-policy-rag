# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the procedures and limits for reimbursement of business expenses incurred by Acme Corp employees. All expenses must be legitimate, necessary, and reasonable business expenses.

## 2. Eligible Expenses

The following categories of expenses are reimbursable with appropriate documentation:

### 2.1 Travel
- Airfare (economy class for domestic; economy or premium economy for flights over 6 hours)
- Ground transportation (taxi, rideshare, rental car, public transit)
- Hotel accommodations (see per diem limits below)
- Parking and tolls

### 2.2 Meals
- Meals while traveling for business
- Working meals with clients, partners, or team members (must include attendee list and business purpose)

### 2.3 Other Business Expenses
- Conference registration fees
- Business-related subscriptions or software (with manager approval)
- Office supplies for remote work setup (see Remote Work policy for limits)
- Client entertainment (with VP+ approval for amounts over $200)

## 3. Per Diem Rates and Spending Limits

| Category | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hotel (domestic) | $200/night | Exceptions require VP approval |
| Hotel (international) | $300/night | Exceptions require VP approval |
| Meals (domestic) | $75/day | Without receipts, GSA per diem applies |
| Meals (international) | $100/day | Without receipts, GSA per diem applies |
| Team meal | $50/person | |
| Client entertainment | $100/person | VP approval required above $200 total |
| Airfare upgrades | Not reimbursable | Unless medical necessity with documentation |

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are **not** reimbursable:
- Alcohol (unless part of approved client entertainment)
- Personal entertainment, mini-bar charges, in-room movies
- Traffic fines, parking violations
- Spouse/partner travel unless prior VP approval
- First-class airfare (domestic)
- Expenses without receipts over $25

## 5. Submission Process

All expenses must be submitted through Expensify within **30 calendar days** of the expense date. Required documentation:
- Original receipts for all expenses over $25
- Business purpose for each expense
- Attendee names for meals with others

Late submissions (30–60 days) require manager and Finance approval. Expenses over 60 days old will not be reimbursed.

## 6. Payment Timeline

Approved expenses are reimbursed via direct deposit within:
- 5 business days for standard submissions
- 10 business days for international or large submissions (>$2,000)

## 7. Corporate Credit Card

Employees with a corporate credit card must use it for all company travel and large purchases. Personal card use for business expenses should be the exception. Corporate card statements must be reconciled monthly.

## 8. Approval Requirements

| Amount | Approval Required |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Direct manager |
| $500–$2,000 | Manager + Finance review |
| Over $2,000 | Manager + Finance + VP |
