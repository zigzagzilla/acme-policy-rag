# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for Paid Time Off (PTO) for all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corp. PTO is provided to give employees flexibility to manage personal time, vacations, and short-term illness without loss of pay.

## 2. Eligibility

All regular full-time employees (working 30+ hours per week) are eligible for PTO from their first day of employment. Part-time employees working 20–29 hours per week accrue PTO at 50% of the full-time rate.

## 3. PTO Accrual Rates

| Years of Service | Annual PTO Days | Accrual Per Pay Period (Biweekly) |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 year | 15 days | 0.577 days |
| 1–3 years | 18 days | 0.692 days |
| 3–5 years | 22 days | 0.846 days |
| 5+ years | 25 days | 0.962 days |

PTO accrues starting from the employee's first day and is available for use after a 30-day waiting period for new hires.

## 4. PTO Carryover

Employees may carry over up to 10 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Any PTO balance above 10 days will be forfeited on December 31st of each year. Employees are encouraged to plan and take their PTO throughout the year.

## 5. PTO Payout Upon Separation

Upon voluntary resignation with at least two weeks' notice, or upon termination without cause, accrued unused PTO will be paid out at the employee's current hourly rate. PTO is not paid out upon termination for cause.

## 6. Requesting PTO

Employees must submit PTO requests via the HR portal at least:
- **1 business day** in advance for requests of 1–2 days.
- **5 business days** in advance for requests of 3–9 days.
- **15 business days** in advance for requests of 10+ consecutive days.

Manager approval is required for all PTO requests. In peak business periods, PTO may be restricted at manager discretion with advance notice.

## 7. Sick Time Usage

PTO may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or care of an immediate family member who is ill. Employees absent for 3 or more consecutive days due to illness may be required to provide a physician's note.

## 8. Bereavement Leave

In the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling, grandparent), employees are entitled to up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave, separate from PTO. For extended family, up to 2 days are provided.
