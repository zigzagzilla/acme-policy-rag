# Company Holiday Calendar

**Policy ID:** HR-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This document lists all official paid company holidays for Acme Corp employees in the United States for 2024. Employees are entitled to take these days off with full pay.

## 2. 2024 Company Holidays

| Date | Day | Holiday |
|---|---|---|
| January 1 | Monday | New Year's Day |
| January 15 | Monday | Martin Luther King Jr. Day |
| February 19 | Monday | Presidents' Day |
| May 27 | Monday | Memorial Day |
| June 19 | Wednesday | Juneteenth National Independence Day |
| July 4 | Thursday | Independence Day |
| September 2 | Monday | Labor Day |
| November 28 | Thursday | Thanksgiving Day |
| November 29 | Friday | Day After Thanksgiving |
| December 24 | Tuesday | Christmas Eve (half day, closes at noon) |
| December 25 | Wednesday | Christmas Day |
| December 31 | Tuesday | New Year's Eve (half day, closes at noon) |

**Total: 12 paid holidays** (11 full days + 2 half days)

## 3. Floating Holidays

In addition to the fixed holidays above, each employee receives **2 floating holidays** per calendar year to be used at their discretion. Floating holidays:
- Must be used within the 2024 calendar year (no carryover)
- Require manager approval at least 3 business days in advance
- Can be taken as a full or half day

## 4. Holiday Work

Employees required to work on a company holiday will receive:
- Their regular holiday pay, **plus**
- Equivalent time off to be taken within 60 days (flex day)

Holiday work must be pre-approved by a manager.

## 5. Part-Time Employees

Part-time employees (20–29 hours/week) receive holiday pay proportional to their scheduled hours on the holiday. For example, an employee scheduled for 6 hours on a given day receives 6 hours of holiday pay.

## 6. International Employees

Employees based outside the United States follow their country's national holiday schedule as determined by HR and provided in a separate country-specific addendum.

## 7. Holiday Pay for New Employees

New employees are eligible for holiday pay from their first day of employment. There is no waiting period for holiday pay.

## 8. Observance of Weekend Holidays

If a holiday falls on a Saturday, it is typically observed on the preceding Friday. If a holiday falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday. Acme Corp will communicate specific observance dates in advance via company-wide announcements.

Contact HR at hr@acmecorp.com with any questions about the holiday schedule.
