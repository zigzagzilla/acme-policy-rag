# Performance Review Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-007  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines Acme Corp's approach to evaluating and managing employee performance. Regular, fair, and constructive performance reviews help employees grow, align individual goals with company objectives, and inform compensation decisions.

## 2. Review Cycle

Acme Corp uses a **bi-annual review cycle**:
- **Mid-Year Check-In**: July 1–31 (informal, no ratings)
- **Annual Review**: December 1–31 (formal, includes ratings and compensation decisions)

In addition, managers are encouraged to conduct brief quarterly conversations about progress, blockers, and development opportunities.

## 3. Annual Review Process

### Step 1: Employee Self-Assessment (November 15–30)
Employees complete a self-assessment in Workday covering:
- Accomplishments and key contributions
- Progress against goals set at the start of the year
- Development areas and growth
- Goals proposed for the next year

### Step 2: Manager Assessment (December 1–15)
Managers complete an assessment for each direct report covering the same dimensions, then meet with employees for a review conversation.

### Step 3: Calibration (December 10–20)
HR facilitates calibration sessions where managers align on ratings across teams to ensure consistency and fairness.

### Step 4: Compensation Decisions (December)
Rating outcomes inform merit increases and bonus payments, effective January 1 of the following year.

## 4. Performance Ratings

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| 5 - Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all expectations; significant impact beyond role scope |
| 4 - Exceeds Expectations | Frequently exceeds expectations; delivers above what is required |
| 3 - Meets Expectations | Consistently meets role requirements; solid, reliable performance |
| 2 - Developing | Partially meets expectations; improvement needed in key areas |
| 1 - Below Expectations | Does not meet basic requirements; Performance Improvement Plan initiated |

The target distribution is approximately: 10% (5), 25% (4), 55% (3), 8% (2), 2% (1).

## 5. Goal Setting

Employees and managers set 3–5 SMART goals at the start of each year via Workday. Goals should be aligned with team and company OKRs. At least one goal should address professional development.

## 6. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)

Employees who receive a rating of 1 (Below Expectations) will be placed on a formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP), which includes:
- Specific, measurable performance gaps identified
- Clear expectations and milestones for the next 60–90 days
- Weekly check-ins with the manager
- HR Business Partner involvement

Employees on a PIP are not eligible for remote work, promotions, or transfers during the PIP period.

## 7. Merit Increases

Merit increases are effective January 1 each year. Guidelines by rating:
- Rating 5: 6–10% increase
- Rating 4: 4–6% increase
- Rating 3: 2–4% increase
- Rating 2: 0–1% increase
- Rating 1: No increase (PIP)

Actual increases are subject to budget availability and manager recommendation.

## 8. Promotions

Promotions may occur during the annual cycle or off-cycle with VP approval. An employee is typically eligible for promotion after 12 months in role and must have a minimum rating of 4 in their most recent annual review.
