# Code of Conduct

**Policy ID:** HR-005  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources / Legal

---

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and inclusive workplace. This Code of Conduct sets out our expectations for employee behavior and the standards we hold ourselves to as an organization.

## 2. Core Values

All employees are expected to embody our core values:

- **Integrity**: Act honestly and ethically in all interactions, even when no one is watching.
- **Respect**: Treat every person — colleagues, clients, and partners — with dignity.
- **Accountability**: Take responsibility for your work and your actions.
- **Collaboration**: Work together and support one another to achieve shared goals.
- **Inclusion**: Welcome diverse perspectives and create an environment where everyone can contribute.

## 3. Workplace Respect and Anti-Harassment

Acme Corp prohibits all forms of harassment, discrimination, and bullying based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, or any other protected characteristic.

Prohibited behaviors include:
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or derogatory remarks
- Unwanted physical contact or sexual advances
- Deliberate exclusion or "freezing out" of colleagues
- Threats or intimidation, whether in person or online (including Slack, email, social media)
- Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict (or appear to conflict) with Acme Corp's interests. You must disclose to HR any:
- Outside employment or consulting that competes with Acme Corp
- Financial interest in a company that does business with Acme Corp
- Personal relationships with individuals you directly supervise or who supervise you

## 5. Confidentiality

Employees must protect Acme Corp's confidential information, including business strategies, customer data, financial information, and intellectual property. This obligation continues after employment ends. Unauthorized disclosure is a serious violation.

## 6. Use of Company Resources

Company resources (computers, phones, credit cards, time) must be used primarily for business purposes. Incidental personal use is acceptable; abuse of company resources will result in disciplinary action.

## 7. Social Media

When posting on personal social media, employees must not:
- Share confidential Acme Corp information
- Make statements that could be interpreted as official Acme Corp positions
- Post content that could embarrass or harm Acme Corp's reputation
- Harass or demean colleagues online

## 8. Reporting Violations

Employees who experience or witness violations of this Code are encouraged to report them promptly. Reports can be made to:
- Your direct manager (if not the subject of the complaint)
- HR Business Partner: hr@acmecorp.com
- Anonymous Ethics Hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS (available 24/7)

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 9. Consequences

Violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action ranging from a written warning to immediate termination, depending on severity. Certain violations may also result in civil or criminal liability.
