# Business Travel Policy

**Policy ID:** FIN-002  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance / HR

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes guidelines for employees who travel on company business. It complements the Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-001) and covers booking procedures, safety requirements, and traveler responsibilities.

## 2. Pre-Trip Authorization

All business travel must be pre-approved before booking:
- **Domestic travel**: Manager approval required. Notify at least 5 business days in advance when possible.
- **International travel**: Manager + VP approval required at least 10 business days in advance.

Emergency travel requiring shorter lead times must still have written manager approval (email is acceptable).

## 3. Booking Requirements

All travel must be booked through the company's approved booking platform, **TravelBank** (travelbank.acmecorp.com), except in documented emergencies.

**Why TravelBank?**
- Ensures compliance with spending limits
- Provides traveler tracking for safety
- Simplifies expense reconciliation

Employees who book outside TravelBank without prior approval may not be reimbursed.

## 4. Air Travel

- **Domestic flights**: Economy class required. Ticket price should not exceed $150 above the lowest available fare at the time of booking.
- **International flights under 6 hours**: Economy class required.
- **International flights over 6 hours**: Premium economy is permitted; business class requires SVP+ approval.
- **Advance booking**: Book at least 14 days in advance when possible to minimize costs. Last-minute bookings require manager justification.

Frequent flyer miles and other loyalty benefits earned on company-paid travel belong to the employee.

## 5. Ground Transportation

- **Rental cars**: Compact or economy class. Decline all optional insurance (company coverage applies through the corporate card). GPS add-ons are not reimbursable.
- **Rideshare/Taxi**: Use for airport transfers and local travel. Document purpose and destination.
- **Personal car**: Reimbursed at the current IRS mileage rate ($0.67/mile for 2024). Employees must carry adequate personal auto insurance.
- **Public transit**: Fully reimbursable with receipt.

## 6. Accommodations

Book hotels through TravelBank. Limits:
- Domestic: $200/night (exceptions require manager approval)
- International: $300/night (exceptions require VP approval)

Hotels must be in a safe area reasonably close to the business destination. Employees may not stay with personal acquaintances and bill lodging expenses.

## 7. Meals During Travel

Meals while traveling for business are reimbursable up to $75/day domestic, $100/day international. All meals require itemized receipts. Alcohol is not reimbursable unless pre-approved as part of client entertainment.

## 8. Travel Safety

Employee safety is the highest priority. Before international travel:
- Register your trip with the State Department (travel.state.gov) for high-risk destinations
- Review the destination safety briefing provided by HR
- Ensure you have emergency contact information for Acme Corp: +1-800-555-1234 (24/7 travel assistance line)

Travel to countries under a U.S. State Department Level 3 or 4 travel advisory requires SVP approval and a security briefing.

## 9. Travel Insurance

Acme Corp provides business travel insurance for all company-approved travel, covering:
- Medical emergencies abroad
- Trip cancellation/interruption due to covered events
- Emergency evacuation

Personal travel added to business trips is not covered. Contact HR for policy details.

## 10. Expense Reporting

All travel expenses must be submitted within 30 days of return via Expensify. Attach all receipts and note the business purpose. See Expense Reimbursement Policy (FIN-001) for full submission guidelines.
