# Employee Benefits Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources / Benefits

---

## 1. Overview

Acme Corp provides a comprehensive benefits package to support employee health, financial well-being, and work-life balance. All benefits are subject to eligibility criteria and applicable plan documents, which govern in the event of any conflict with this summary.

## 2. Health Insurance

### Medical
Acme Corp offers three medical plan options through BlueCross BlueShield:
- **Plan A (HDHP)**: High-deductible plan with HSA eligibility. Company contributes $750/year to HSA (individual) or $1,500/year (family).
- **Plan B (PPO)**: Moderate deductible with broad in-network access.
- **Plan C (HMO)**: Lowest out-of-pocket, requires PCP referrals for specialists.

Company pays **80% of employee-only premiums** and 50% of dependent premiums for all plans.

### Dental
Delta Dental PPO plan. Company pays 80% of employee-only premium. Covers preventive care (100%), basic restorative (80%), major restorative (50%), orthodontia (50%, up to $1,500 lifetime, children only).

### Vision
VSP vision plan. Company pays 100% of employee-only premium. Covers annual eye exam ($10 copay), frames or contacts allowance of $150 per year.

## 3. 401(k) Retirement Plan

Acme Corp offers a 401(k) plan through Fidelity.
- Employees may contribute up to IRS annual limits ($23,000 for 2024; $30,500 if age 50+)
- **Company match**: 100% of the first 4% of salary contributed
- **Vesting**: Immediately vested on day one of employment
- Investment options include index funds, target-date funds, and a brokerage window

## 4. Life and Disability Insurance

- **Basic Life Insurance**: 2x annual salary, company-paid
- **Voluntary Life**: Up to 5x annual salary, employee-paid
- **Short-Term Disability**: 60% of salary for up to 12 weeks, company-paid
- **Long-Term Disability**: 60% of salary after 90 days, company-paid

## 5. Additional Benefits

### Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
Free, confidential counseling and support services for employees and household members, including:
- Up to 8 free therapy sessions per issue per year
- Financial counseling and legal consultation (1 free session)
- 24/7 crisis support hotline

### Parental Leave
- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave
- **Secondary caregiver**: 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave
- Available for birth, adoption, and foster placement
- Must be taken within 12 months of the qualifying event

### Commuter Benefits
Pre-tax commuter benefits up to IRS limits ($315/month for transit, $315/month for parking in 2024) via WageWorks.

### Learning and Development
- **Annual L&D Stipend**: $1,500 per employee per year for courses, conferences, books, or certifications
- Submit requests via the L&D portal; manager approval required for amounts over $500
- Unused stipend does not carry over to the next year

### Wellness Benefit
$500 annual wellness reimbursement for gym memberships, fitness equipment, meditation apps, or wellness services. Submit receipts through Expensify with category "Wellness Benefit."

## 6. Open Enrollment

Open enrollment occurs annually in November for benefits effective January 1. Employees who do not make active elections will be auto-enrolled in their current elections (except for HSA contributions, which must be re-elected annually).
