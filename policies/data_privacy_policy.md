# Data Privacy Policy

**Policy ID:** LEGAL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Legal / Compliance

---

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp is committed to protecting the privacy of the personal data it processes—belonging to employees, customers, partners, and others. This policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data in compliance with applicable privacy laws including GDPR, CCPA, and other relevant regulations.

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

Acme Corp collects personal data in the course of its operations, including:

**Employee Data**: Name, contact information, job title, salary, bank account (for payroll), performance records, benefits elections, and HR correspondence.

**Customer Data**: Name, contact information, billing details, purchase history, and support interactions.

**Website Visitors**: IP addresses, cookies, and usage analytics (via opt-in consent mechanisms).

## 3. How We Use Personal Data

Personal data is processed only for legitimate business purposes, including:
- Employment administration (payroll, benefits, performance management)
- Service delivery and customer support
- Legal and compliance obligations
- Security monitoring and fraud prevention

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We do not use personal data for automated decision-making that has significant legal effects without human review.

## 4. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|---|---|
| Employee records (HR) | Duration of employment + 7 years |
| Payroll records | 7 years (IRS requirement) |
| Customer purchase data | 5 years after last transaction |
| Support tickets | 3 years |
| Website analytics | 13 months |
| Application/security logs | 12 months |

Data is securely deleted or anonymized after the retention period expires.

## 5. Employee Rights

Employees have rights regarding their personal data:
- **Access**: Request a copy of personal data Acme Corp holds about you
- **Correction**: Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Deletion**: Request deletion (subject to legal retention obligations)
- **Portability**: Receive data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, submit a written request to privacy@acmecorp.com. Requests are fulfilled within 30 days.

## 6. Data Security

All personal data is protected by the controls described in the Information Security Policy (IT-001), including encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, and audit logging. Access to personal data is restricted to employees with a legitimate business need.

## 7. Third-Party Processors

Some personal data is shared with vetted third-party service providers (e.g., payroll processor, benefits administrator, cloud infrastructure). All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements that require them to protect data to Acme Corp's standards.

## 8. International Data Transfers

For transfers of personal data from the EU/EEA or UK to the United States or other countries, Acme Corp uses appropriate safeguards including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or relies on other lawful transfer mechanisms.

## 9. Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting personal data, Acme Corp will notify affected individuals and regulators within the timeframes required by applicable law (e.g., 72 hours under GDPR). See IT-001 for incident reporting procedures.

## 10. Questions and Contact

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights: privacy@acmecorp.com or Legal, Acme Corp, 123 Tech Lane, San Francisco, CA 94105.
