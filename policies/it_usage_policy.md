# IT Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy ID:** IT-002  
**Effective Date:** March 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Information Technology

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the acceptable use of all information technology resources owned or managed by Acme Corp. It supplements the Information Security Policy (IT-001) and applies to all employees, contractors, and authorized users.

## 2. Company-Owned Devices

### 2.1 Laptops and Desktops
All employees are issued a company laptop. Employees must:
- Keep operating systems and software updated (auto-updates must remain enabled)
- Not modify system settings, disable security software, or remove MDM profiles
- Secure their device when leaving it unattended (lock screen or physical lock)
- Return equipment in good condition upon termination

### 2.2 Mobile Devices
Company mobile phones may be issued to employees in roles that require them. Personal devices used for company email or Slack must be enrolled in the company MDM (Jamf).

## 3. Software and Licensing

Only IT-approved software may be installed on company devices. To request new software:
1. Submit a request via the IT Service Desk portal (it.acmecorp.com)
2. Include a business justification
3. IT will review for security and licensing compliance within 3 business days

Employees must not use unlicensed, pirated, or "cracked" software on company devices.

## 4. Internet and Network Use

The company network and internet connection are primarily for business use. Prohibited activities include:
- Accessing or downloading illegal content
- Using peer-to-peer file sharing networks
- Streaming video or audio for personal entertainment during work hours (bandwidth-intensive)
- Attempting to bypass web filters or security controls
- Using personal VPNs that circumvent company security monitoring

## 5. Email and Communication

Company email accounts (user@acmecorp.com) are for business use. Employees should not:
- Auto-forward company email to personal accounts
- Use personal email accounts for business communications involving sensitive information
- Send confidential company data (financial, legal, customer data) via unencrypted channels

Email retention: Company email is retained for 7 years per legal and compliance requirements. Employees should not expect privacy in company email communications.

## 6. Cloud Services and SaaS

Employees must not sign up for or use cloud services to store or process company data without IT approval. Approved cloud services include: Google Workspace, Slack, Salesforce, Zoom, GitHub (company account), and AWS/GCP (via IT-managed accounts).

Using personal accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox) to store or share company data is prohibited.

## 7. IT Support and Help Desk

For IT assistance:
- **Portal**: it.acmecorp.com (for non-urgent requests)
- **Email**: ithelp@acmecorp.com
- **Phone**: ext. 5555 (urgent issues during business hours)
- **After-hours emergencies**: security@acmecorp.com

Standard response times: Critical issues — 2 hours; High — 4 hours; Medium — 1 business day; Low — 3 business days.

## 8. Monitoring

Acme Corp reserves the right to monitor, inspect, and audit the use of all company IT resources. Employees should have no expectation of privacy on company systems or networks. Monitoring may include email content, web browsing, and application usage logs.
