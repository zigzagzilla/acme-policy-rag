# Employee Onboarding Guide

**Policy ID:** HR-010  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Welcome to Acme Corp

We're excited to have you join the team! This guide walks you through everything you need to do in your first 30 days to get set up, meet the team, and start contributing.

## 2. Before Your First Day

You will receive a welcome email from HR at least 3 business days before your start date containing:
- Your company email address (user@acmecorp.com)
- Instructions to complete pre-hire paperwork (I-9, W-4, direct deposit) via Workday
- A first-day schedule and manager contact information
- Instructions to set up MFA on your company account

**IT Equipment**: Your laptop will be shipped to your home (remote employees) or available at the front desk (on-site/hybrid employees) on your first day.

## 3. First Day

### Morning (9:00 AM)
- Meet with your manager for an orientation meeting
- Receive your laptop and set up your accounts
- Complete IT onboarding: install required software, set up VPN, connect to Slack

### Required First-Day Setups
1. **Google Workspace**: Log in at workspace.google.com
2. **Slack**: Download the app and join #general, #announcements, and your team channel
3. **HR Systems**: Complete remaining onboarding tasks in Workday
4. **Password Manager**: Install and set up 1Password (license provided by IT)
5. **VPN**: Install GlobalProtect and test the connection

## 4. First Week Milestones

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | IT setup, meet manager and immediate team |
| Day 2 | Benefits enrollment (must complete within 5 days) |
| Day 3 | Mandatory training: Security Awareness, Code of Conduct |
| Day 4–5 | Meet cross-functional stakeholders, review team roadmap |

## 5. Benefits Enrollment

New employees must enroll in benefits within **5 business days** of their start date via Workday. Benefits available include:
- Medical, dental, and vision insurance (company covers 80% of employee premium)
- 401(k) plan (company matches up to 4% of salary, vesting on day one)
- Life insurance and short/long-term disability (company paid)
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): free confidential counseling and support
- Optional: FSA/HSA, commuter benefits, supplemental life insurance

Employees who miss the 5-day enrollment window must wait until the next open enrollment period unless they have a qualifying life event.

## 6. Mandatory Training

All new employees must complete the following training within 30 days of hire:
1. **Security Awareness Training** (IT) — ~45 minutes, via KnowBe4
2. **Code of Conduct Training** (HR) — ~30 minutes, via Workday Learning
3. **Anti-Harassment Training** (HR) — ~60 minutes, required by state law
4. **Data Privacy Training** (Legal) — ~30 minutes

Completion is tracked automatically. Incomplete training will result in system access restrictions after 30 days.

## 7. First 30/60/90 Day Plan

Your manager will work with you to set goals for your first 90 days. A typical framework:
- **Day 30**: Understand the team, systems, and immediate priorities
- **Day 60**: Begin contributing to projects with increasing autonomy
- **Day 90**: Delivering independent work; first performance check-in with manager
