# Remote Work Policy

**Policy ID:** HR-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Human Resources

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy provides guidelines for employees who work remotely, whether on a full-time, part-time, or occasional basis. Acme Corp supports flexible work arrangements while maintaining productivity, security, and team cohesion.

## 2. Eligibility

Remote work eligibility is determined based on:
- Job function suitability (roles requiring physical presence are not eligible)
- Performance standing (employees on a Performance Improvement Plan are not eligible for remote work)
- Demonstrated ability to work independently
- Manager and HR approval

New employees must complete their first 90 days of onboarding in-person or in a hybrid arrangement before transitioning to fully remote.

## 3. Work Arrangements

### 3.1 Hybrid Work
The standard work model is hybrid, with employees expected to be on-site a minimum of **2 days per week** (typically Tuesday and Thursday, as coordinated with the team). Specific on-site days are determined by the manager and team.

### 3.2 Full Remote
Full remote arrangements are available for roles specifically designated as remote-eligible or with VP approval. Remote employees are expected to be available during core hours of 10am–3pm in their manager's time zone.

### 3.3 Occasional Remote
Employees may request to work remotely on an ad-hoc basis with manager approval. These requests should be made via the HR portal at least 1 business day in advance.

## 4. Home Office Requirements

Employees working remotely are responsible for maintaining an appropriate work environment including:
- Reliable high-speed internet (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload)
- Quiet, professional space for video calls
- Ergonomic setup suitable for extended computer use

Acme Corp provides a **one-time home office stipend of $500** for full-time remote employees to cover equipment such as a monitor, keyboard, or chair. Receipts must be submitted through Expensify within 90 days of approval.

## 5. Equipment and Technology

Company-issued laptops and peripherals are provided. Employees are responsible for the security and proper use of company equipment. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported immediately.

Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely. See the Information Security Policy (IT-001) for full remote access requirements.

## 6. Availability and Communication

Remote employees are expected to:
- Be available via Slack and email during their scheduled working hours
- Respond to messages within 2 hours during core hours (10am–3pm local manager time)
- Have their camera on during team meetings unless bandwidth issues prevent it
- Update their Slack status to reflect availability

## 7. Performance Expectations

Remote work does not change job expectations or performance standards. Managers will conduct regular check-ins (minimum weekly) with remote employees. Remote work arrangements can be revoked if performance declines.

## 8. Tax and Legal

Employees working remotely from a state different from their official work location must notify HR in advance. Multi-state arrangements may have tax and legal implications and require HR review before approval.
